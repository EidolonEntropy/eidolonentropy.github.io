3D Technical Artist Call

Help bring TAKEOVER dragons into 3D!

Takeover is looking for Blender artists who can assist in any of the following categories:

  • Cartoony quadruped rigging
  • Overlapping scale modeling techniques
  • Formatting a model for reuse with parameter tweaks for variation
  • Rigging wings

Even if you only know one thing from the above list, we want to hear from YOU!

This project aims to produce one base model and rig that can be reused with slight tweaks to create additional characters based off of it. You can help in any way you prefer, be that as an advisor or in directly working on the model hands-on. I am also looking to learn from your processes so that I can be a better 3D artist myself moving forward.

This model is being built in Blender with a final destination of Unreal Engine, so it is much appreciated if your skills are geared towards those programs, but if you can provide higher level advice program affinity doesn't matter. Note that Takeover is being made with non-photorealistic rendering (NPR) in mind, characters will be lower poly, cell/cartoon shaded, and make use of line art in renders; think Guilty Gear Strive or Genshin Impact. The stylised 2D artwork will be converted into 3D very closely.
You will be compensated for your contributions, which we can discuss upon contact. I don't have a very hefty sum to hand out but I want to say thanks however I can.

Rigging

After the experience of poorly rigging numerous characters for a short film, I have learned that my skills are not sufficient to create a workable animation rig that will stand the test of time. If you're confident that you can make a quadrupedal rig with IK that's capable of cartoonish posing, I want your help with making a rig for these dragons!
I am used to handling facial animations through a blendshape rig that works well enough for my uses, so facial rigging for this role is OPTIONAL. If you also have a really good method for facial rigs you'd be willing to share with the project, feel free to offer that too!

Scale Modeling

Half of the Takeover dragon varieties have large overlapping scute-like scales along their bodies. I have zero clue how to make these in 3D.
Takeover uses a cartoony style, so if the simplest answer is a series of planes I see no problem there. The next question would be how to rig these planes so that they bend according to the body without clipping through each other. I suppose something procedural would work well here, but I'm really quite clueless on the matter; any of the above might be entirely the wrong way to go about it! I require your expertise, any tips you can contribute would help immensely!

Reusable Model Design

Takeover features four primary types of dragon with very little variation in anatomy, and different individuals vary only slightly in body plan. I figure once the first model is made it could be easily converted to cover the other variations, and different individuals could be made from a series of sliders adjusting different physical characteristics such as length, height, and body shape. Aside from the prediction that blendshapes are probably involved, I've no idea how to go about this system.
I want to consult with you throughout the process to ensure things are being built with this reuse in mind in addition to the process of implementing the system. Alternatiely, just sending a quality video tutorial you know of is equally appreciated!

Rigging Wings

I've watched many a tutorial on how to make batlike wings in 3D. I am yet to make a working model that's not full of jank.
The good news is these dragons need only a series of planes with a bit of shaping at the end for their wings. The bad news is it'd be nice to have them respond to physics, mostly in the realms of drag and collision. If you have experience with making wings of this type, I need your wisdom on the matter.
